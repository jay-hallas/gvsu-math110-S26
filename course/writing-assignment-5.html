    Worksheet Writing Assignment 5: Pattern Types, Quadratic Forms, and Revenue Models
    

    This assignment asks you to classify patterns from data, move between the three forms of a quadratic, and interpret a quadratic revenue model in context.
    🔗
    Show every algebraic step. A final answer with no supporting work earns no credit.
    🔗
    In Question 1, your justification must include the actual differences or ratios you computed, not just the name of the pattern.
    🔗
    In Question 2, show which point you substituted each time you solve for the leading coefficient.
    🔗
    Every answer that asks for an interpretation should be written in a complete sentence, in context, with units.
    🔗
    Upload a clean, high-quality scan of your pages to Blackboard when complete.
